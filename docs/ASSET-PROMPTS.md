# Original project artwork

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool; no reference artwork was copied. Final assets live in this project's assets directory.

## assets/garden.png

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: original high-detail background for a premium Indian wedding invitation mobile website. Create a portrait 2:3 illustration, no text or letters. A symmetrical intricately carved warm ivory sandstone cusped palace arch framing a luminous pale champagne sky and distant misty garden. The arch occupies the top 16% and thin side columns the outer 10% each side, carved botanical relief. Fine delicate sage-green jasmine vines hang softly inward from the arch. Large central area from 20% to 65% is quiet very pale warm ivory blank atmospheric space for text. Lower quarter: a tranquil garden lake with two elegant white swans facing each other, reflections on water, detailed ivory and pale blush roses, tiny daisies and sage foliage at the two bottom corners. Soft painterly watercolor realism with extremely fine carved detail, romantic gilded sunlight, restrained antique gold ivory sage palette, no pink sky. Seamlessly fades to plain warm parchment #f9f0e0 at bottom. Composition built for centered wedding calligraphy overlay. Original artwork, no logo, no typography, no watermark.

## assets/envelope.png

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: straight-on wedding envelope texture for an animated website, portrait 9:16. Full-bleed edge-to-edge single closed warm ivory cotton paper envelope, with finely embossed ivory-white jasmine stems and tiny flowers running vertically along both side edges and on the triangular flap. Four triangular flaps, upper triangular flap goes from the top two corners to the exact center of the image, bottom triangle from bottom corners to exact center, shallow realistic shadows along folds. The entire image is the envelope, absolutely no margin around it, no table or props. Elegant photoreal fine fibrous paper, soft cream palette, delicate raised tone-on-tone botanical relief like luxury letterpress stationery. Center where all four flaps meet must be empty because a separate wax seal will be rendered over it in code. No wax seal, no letters, no text, no typography, no watermark. Perfect centered symmetric composition.

## assets/floral.png

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: transparent botanical foreground decoration for a luxury wedding invitation website. One richly detailed airy curved floral spray with fine sage and olive green leaves, graceful jasmine vines, ivory garden roses, one pale blush peony and tiny cream daisies. Bouquet dense at lower left quarter, delicate long tapering tendrils rise up left edge and curve toward upper right, an L-shaped corner ornament. Watercolor and vintage botanical illustration with realistic delicate layered petals, warm soft light, muted elegant ivory blush sage palette. Full cutout with genuine alpha transparent background, no paper rectangle, no lettering, no border, no shadow rectangle. Portrait 2:3 composition. Crisp separate leaves and fine stems.

SVG monogram, parchment texture, ornament and event motifs were authored in code.

The site consumes optimized `assets/garden.webp`, `assets/envelope.webp` and `assets/floral.webp` versions; the generated PNG originals are retained alongside them. Format conversion preserves the transparent floral alpha. `assets/venue.jpg` is the user's supplied venue image and was not generated or retouched here.
